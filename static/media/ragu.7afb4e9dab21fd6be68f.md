# Ragù alla Bolognese

#pasta #lunch #dinner #italian

## Ingredients
For two with leftovers
- 1 Big Carrot
- Celery
- 1 Onion
- 300gr 50/50 Ground Beef and Pork
- 500ml Beef Stock
- Tomato Paste
- Red Wine
- Oil and Butter

## Food Prep
- Dice the carrot, onion and celery

## Preparation
1. Start with the Soffritto:
    - Melt some butter with oil until combined
    - Cook the carrot, onion and celery until soft
2. Add the ground meat and cook until dryer
3. Add one glass of red wine and cook until the alcohol has evaporated
4. Mix the beef stock with the tomato paste and add to the meat
5. Cover and let simmer for at least 2h, the longer the better
6. Remove the lid and let the sauce reduce at the end
7. Cook the pasta and add it to the sauce
8. Serve with grated cheese
