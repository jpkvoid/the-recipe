# Vegetarian Tartare

#lunch #dinner #starter #vegetarian 
## Ingredients
- Cucumber
- Tomato
- Feta
- Lemon Juice
- Mustard
- Egg Yolk
- Olive Oil
- Parsley
- Chives
- Shallot

## Preparation
- Slice the tomatoes, cucumber and as small as wanted and mix them together to form a tartare like mixture
- Finely chop the shallot and add it to the mix
- Slice the herbs and add them
- Mix in the seasoning ingredients and adapt the quantities to reach the desired taste
- Serve with toasted bread
