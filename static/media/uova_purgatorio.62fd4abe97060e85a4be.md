# Uova in Purgatorio

#brunch #vegetarian #dinner #italian
## Ingredients
- Eggs
- Peeled Tomatoes
- Onion
- Garlic
- Olive Oil
- Basil
- Parmigiano / Pecorino
- Pepper

## Preparation
1. Cook the garlic for a few minutes on medium heat to infuse the oil with garlic - don't overcook the garlic
2. Remove the garlic and cook the chopped onion until translucent
3. Add the peeled tomatoes, rince off the can with water and add to the pan
4. Add some basil leaves and cook until reduced to the right consistency
5. With a ladle gently poach the eggs in the sauce and cook until ready
6. Serve with toasted bread
