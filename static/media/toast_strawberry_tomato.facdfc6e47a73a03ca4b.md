# Strawberry, Tomato, Mint, Green Pea Toast

#brunch #vegetarian #vegan

## Ingredients
- Cherry Tomatoes
- Strawberries
- Bread
- Green Peas
- Spring Onions
- Mint
- Olive Oil
- Salt and Pepper
- Lemon

## Making the Purée
1. Cook the peas with the spring onions until bright green
2. Transfer to a blender or mortar with the mint
3. Blend until smooth

## Making the Topping
1. Cut the tomatoes and strawberries into similar sized bits
2. Mix with olive oil, salt and pepper

## Serving
1. Toast the bread slices
2. Spread the Purée
3. Add the topping
4. Sprinkle some spring onions slices
5. Sprinkle with lemon juice
