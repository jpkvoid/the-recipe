# Pizza Bianca Spinach Lemon and Mint

#pizza #lunch #dinner #italian #vegetarian

## Ingredients
- Pizza Dough
- 100gr Ricotta
- 50gr White Cheese
- 200gr Spinach
- 1 bulb Mozzarella
- 1 Lemon
- Fresh Basil and Mint
- Olive Oil
- Pepper and Salt

## Preparation
1. Put some water to boil in a kettle for the spinach
2. Pour the boiling water on the spinach in a colander
3. Zest the lemon
4. Juice half the lemon
5. Mix the ricotta, white cheese, lemon and 1 table spoon per person of olive oil
6. Dry and cut the mozzarella in thin slices
7. Spread the cheese mixture on the dough
8. Add the mozzarella
9. Add the spinach, pressing it to get the water out
10. Cook
11. Add the basil, mint and lemon zest
