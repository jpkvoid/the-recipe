# Teriyaki Tofu/Tempeh/Beef

#lunch #dinner #japanese #pokebowl #noodles #baobun

## Ingredients
- Teriyaki Sauce
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Water
- Tofu/Tempeh/Beefsteak
- White Wine Vinegar

## Preparation
1. Grate the ginger and chop the garlic
2. Mix with the teriyaki sauce, vinegar and a bit of water in a bowl
3. Cut the beef in thin strips or tofu into cubes
4. Marinate the tofu/beef in the sauce in the fridge or just while you prepare the rest of your dish
5. Pan fry for a short moment not too overcook the beef
6. Serve with poke bowls, bao buns or wraps
