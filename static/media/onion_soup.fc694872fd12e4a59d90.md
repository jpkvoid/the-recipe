# Onion Soup

#lunch #dinner #starter #french #vegetarian

## Ingredients
- 3 Onions
- 400ml Stock/p.
- Oil
- Salt and Pepper

## Food Prep
- Prepare the stock
- Slice the onions into half rings

## Preparation
1. Heat the oil
2. Add the onions and cook until the deposits are hard to scoop with the spoon - add salt to help get the water out of the onions
3. Deglaze with stock - repeat a few times
4. Add 400ml of stock per person and let it simmer for at least 30 minutes
5. Season with pepper
6. Serve with croutons and cheese
