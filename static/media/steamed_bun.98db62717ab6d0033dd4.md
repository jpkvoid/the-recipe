# Steamed Bun / Bao Bun

#bread #breakfast #lunch #dinner 

## Ingredients
- 420gr Flour
- 250ml Warm Water
- 7gr Instant Yeast
- 1 teaspoon Baking Powder
- Sesame Oil

## Making the Dough
- Combine the flour, salt and baking powder
- Knead until it forms a shaggy ball
- Cover and let it rest for 10 minutes
- Knead until the dough is smooth, supple and elastic
- Cover and let rest for 1h to 1h30, or in the fridge for 8 hours, until
the dough doubled in size
- Knead another 5 minutes before shaping
- Divide into equal parts

## Bao Buns
- Roll into a long, oval shape
- Brush with oil so it doesn’t stick when folding
- Fold in half
- Put in steamer basket, cover and let rest for 10 minutes
- Steam for 10 minutes
- Remove steamer from steam, but don’t open, let rest for 10 minutes
- Add vegetables
- Add protein, like tofu or beef teriyaki

After steaming, the buns can be kept in the freezer. Just steam again for next time.
