# Bruschetta

#breakfast #lunch #starter #italian #vegetarian #vegan

## Ingredients
-  Tomatoes
- Bread
- Garlic
- Olive Oil
- Basil
- Salt and Pepper

## Preparation
1. Dice the tomatoes
2. Mix with olive oil, salt and pepper
3. Toast the bread
4. Rub the garlic on the toasted bread
5. Chop the remaining garlic and add to the tomato mix
6. Spread the tomatoes on the bread
