# Tabouleh

#lunch #dinner #mezze #salad #lebanese #levantine #vegetarian #vegan

## Ingredients
- Curly Parsley
- Mint
- Tomatoes
- Spring Onion
- Fine Bulgur
- Olive Oil
- Lemon Juice
- Salt and Pepper

## Preparation
1. Add the oil and lemon juice to the bulgur, mix and let soak while preparing the rest
2. Cut the parsley and mint
3. Dice the tomatoes
4. Cut the spring onion
5. Mix all the ingredients together and add the bulgur-lemon-oil seasoning
6. Serve with pita bread, falafel, hummus and other mezzes
