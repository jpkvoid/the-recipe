# Chicken and Lemon Stew

#lunch #dinner #soup

## Ingredients
- 2 Onions
- 2 Large Potatoes
- 200gr Chicken Thigh
- Dried Terragon
- Flour
- 1L Stock
- 2 Lemons
- 60ml Soy Sauce
- Baby Corn
- 150ml Cooking Cream
- Butter
- Olive Oil
- Salt and Pepper

## Food Prep
- Finely chop the onions
- Peel and dice the potatoes
- Prepare the stock
- Zest and juice the lemons
- Drain and roughly chop the corn
- Dice the chicken

## Preparation
1. Melt the butter with the oil
2. Cook the onion until translucent
3. Add the potatoes and cook for 2-3 minutes
4. Add the chicken and herbs and cook for 5 minutes
5. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute
6. Add the stock, lemon zest, lemon juice, soy sauce and corn
7. Cover and cook until the chicken is cooked through
8. Remove from heat and stir in the cream
9. Serve with toasted bread rubbed with garlic
