# Carbonara

#pasta #lunch #dinner #italian

## Ingredients
For two
- 150gr Guanciale
- 150gr Pecorino
- 3 Eggs
- Pasta of Choice

## Preparation
1. Start cooking the pasta
2. While the pasta is cooking cut the guanciale into thin pieces and cook on medium heat
3. Beat the eggs and mix in grated pecorino until it combines into a thick paste
4. Keep some of the cooking water and fry the cooked pasta with the guanciale for a minute
5. Turn the heat off, add the egg and cheese mixture and some of the cooking water to make it creamy
6. Season with pepper and serve with grated cheese
