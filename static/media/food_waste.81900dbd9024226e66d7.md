# Tip: Food Waste

#tips #foodwaste 

## Vegetable Stock
Save and freeze the scraps of onions, carrots, spring onions, garlic, radishes.
Avoid potatoes and cabbage. When gathered enough, make a veg-
etable stock by boiling the scraps at least 30 minutes. Save up to 4 days
in the fridge and up to 3 months in the freezer.
