# Tavče Gravče

#dinner #macedonian #vegetarian #vegan

## Ingredients
- White Beans
- Onion
- Paprika Powder
- Flour
- Oil
- Bay Leaf
- Vegetable Stock

## Food Prep
- Soak the beans overnight (or anywhere between 3 and 12 hours)
- Wash the beans
- Finely chop the onion

## Preparation
1. Boil the beans and onions in plenty of water with a bay leaf and some begetable stock for about 2 hours
2. Make a roux:
    - Mix equal part oil and flour
    - Cook until it gets darker, but not too dark
    - Add paprika powder
3. Add the roux to the beans and stir
4. transfer the beans to an oven dish
5. Add a bell pepper on top of the beans
6. Bake in the oven for 30 minutes to 1h at 200 degrees or until a thin crust forms on top
