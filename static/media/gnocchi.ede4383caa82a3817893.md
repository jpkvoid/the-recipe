# Gnocchi

#pasta #lunch #dinner #italian #vegetarian #vegan 

## Ingredients
- Potatoes
- Flour
- Olive Oil

## Making the Dough
1. Cook the potatoes with the skin for about 1h
2. Peel the potatoes, still warm, with a clean towel and paring knife
3. Mash the potatoes with a fork
4. Incorporate the flour and knead until at the right consistency

## Shaping the Gnocchi
1. Divide the dough into smaller parts
2. Roll into a stick shape of the thickness wanted for the gnocchi
3. Cut to the desired size
4. *Optional* Roll the gnocchi individually on a fork to make the ridges

## Cooking the Gnocchi
Cook the gnocchi in boiling water until they float (about 1 minute)
