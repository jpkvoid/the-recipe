# Hummus

#dip #mezze #levantine #lebanese #mortar #lunch #brunch

## Ingredients
- Chick Peas
- Sesame Seeds
- Olive Oil
- Lemon
- Cold Water

## Preparation
1. Soak the chick peas overnight
2. Cook the chick peas
3. Make the tahini:
	1. Roast the sesame seeds
	2. Grind in the mortar until it's a paste
	3. Slowly add olive oil
	4. Adjust consistency with some water if needed
4. Reserve the tahini aside
5. Grind the chick peas in the mortar
6. Slowly add olive oil
7. Add the tahini
8. Add lemon juice
9. At the end add a little bit of cold water to make it creamier
